A keepsake generated in your browser, not an official record.

Your name is never sent anywhere. To fetch history, your birthplace city and birth date go to Open-Meteo (location and weather) and Wikipedia (events). Heartbeat and breath totals use fixed averages (70 beats and 15 breaths a minute) and are illustrations, not medical data. The moon phase is calculated with the astronomy-engine library, and falls back to an approximation if it can’t load.
